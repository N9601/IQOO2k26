Threat coverage

Each fraud pattern that drains Indian D2C, and the specific Truthbox mechanism that defeats it. Nothing here is aspirational: every mechanism is implemented in this app and provable on the Verify page.

What Truthbox does not claim

Truthbox does not claim to detect every possible fraud. A determined buyer can film a genuinely staged scene. What it removes is the evidence vacuum that makes cheap fraud free: it raises the cost of a fraudulent claim from "re-encode a WhatsApp video" to "defeat live on-device vision during a continuous, hardware-attested capture bound to the order before the box was opened." That shift is what moves 3-7% of GMV back onto the right side of the ledger.
